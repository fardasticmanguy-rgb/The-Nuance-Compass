# The Nuance Compass — Full Question Bank (113 questions)

## Answer options (same for every question)

| Option | Value | Slider range |
|---|---|---|
| Strongly disagree | -90 | −100 to −73 |
| Disagree | -55 | −72 to −23 |
| Neutral | 0 | −22 to +22 |
| Agree | 55 | +23 to +72 |
| Strongly agree | 90 | +73 to +100 |

You can also drag a fine slider anywhere from −100 to +100. It snaps to the five options when within 8 points.

After answering, caveats (nuances) appear:
- **Agree (> +4):** "You agree. On what condition?" shows agree caveats, then any "any" caveats. Disagree caveats are offered as "Anything from the other side you would still sign up to?"
- **Disagree (< −4):** "You disagree. With any reservation?" shows disagree caveats, then any "any" caveats. Agree caveats are offered as the cross-side.
- **Neutral (−4 to +4):** all caveats are shown together.

## Axes

Positive weight pushes toward the right pole, negative toward the left.

| Key | Axis | Left (−) | Right (+) |
|---|---|---|---|
| econ | Economics | Equality | Markets |
| auth | Authority | Liberty | Order |
| cult | Culture | Tradition | Progress |
| natl | Borders | Nation | World |
| ecol | Ecology | Growth | Nature |
| govn | Decision | The People | The Experts |

# Money & Work (18)

_Who owns what, and what a person is owed._

### 1. Nobody should have to earn the right to food, shelter and medicine.

- **ID:** `w01`
- **Explanation:** This asks whether survival itself should depend on having a job. Agreeing points toward a guaranteed floor beneath everyone; disagreeing points toward support being tied to contribution.
- **Scoring (agreeing):** Economics −0.9 (→ Equality), Authority +0.15 (→ Order)
- **Caveats:**
  - _If you agree:_ "…but the floor should be bare, not comfortable" → Economics +0.3 (→ Markets)
  - _If you agree:_ "…and it should be paid for by taxing wealth, not wages" → Economics −0.35 (→ Equality)
  - _If you disagree:_ "…unless the person genuinely cannot work" → Economics −0.3 (→ Equality)
  - _Any answer:_ "…charity and community should do this, not the state" → Economics +0.35 (→ Markets), Authority −0.4 (→ Liberty)

### 2. If the market pays someone a fortune, they have probably earned it.

- **ID:** `w02`
- **Explanation:** This is about whether market prices reflect real worth. Agreeing treats wealth as roughly deserved; disagreeing treats it as an accident of luck, birth or power.
- **Scoring (agreeing):** Economics +0.85 (→ Markets), Culture −0.2 (→ Tradition)
- **Caveats:**
  - _If you agree:_ "…but not when the fortune comes from inheritance" → Economics −0.3 (→ Equality)
  - _If you agree:_ "…but not when the industry is propped up by the state" → Economics +0.2 (→ Markets), Decision −0.2 (→ The People)
  - _If you disagree:_ "…though effort and risk still deserve some reward" → Economics +0.3 (→ Markets)

### 3. The people who do the work should have a real say in how the company is run.

- **ID:** `w03`
- **Explanation:** This covers everything from unions to workers sitting on company boards. Agreeing means power inside a firm should be shared; disagreeing means the owners decide.
- **Scoring (agreeing):** Economics −0.75 (→ Equality), Authority −0.15 (→ Liberty)
- **Caveats:**
  - _If you agree:_ "…by law, not by the goodwill of owners" → Economics −0.25 (→ Equality), Authority +0.35 (→ Order)
  - _If you agree:_ "…only in large firms, not small businesses" → Economics +0.25 (→ Markets)
  - _If you disagree:_ "…though they should at least be able to organise freely" → Economics −0.3 (→ Equality), Authority −0.3 (→ Liberty)

### 4. Large inherited fortunes are a problem worth fixing.

- **ID:** `w04`
- **Explanation:** Inheritance passes advantage between generations without anyone earning it. Agreeing favours taxing or limiting it; disagreeing treats what you leave your children as nobody else's business.
- **Scoring (agreeing):** Economics −0.8 (→ Equality), Culture +0.25 (→ Progress)
- **Caveats:**
  - _If you agree:_ "…but family homes and small businesses should be left alone" → Economics +0.3 (→ Markets)
  - _If you disagree:_ "…though the very largest estates are a fair target" → Economics −0.3 (→ Equality)

### 5. Essential services like water and transport are better run by private companies than by the state.

- **ID:** `w05`
- **Explanation:** The privatisation argument. Agreeing trusts competition to deliver essentials more cheaply; disagreeing thinks things everyone needs should be run for use rather than profit.
- **Scoring (agreeing):** Economics +0.85 (→ Markets)
- **Caveats:**
  - _If you disagree:_ "…but run locally, not from a distant capital" → Authority −0.4 (→ Liberty), Decision −0.2 (→ The People)
  - _If you disagree:_ "…public ownership, but managed at arm's length from politicians" → Decision +0.45 (→ The Experts)
  - _If you agree:_ "…though they need tight regulation either way" → Economics −0.3 (→ Equality), Decision +0.25 (→ The Experts)

### 6. What a company pays its executives is nobody's business but the company's.

- **ID:** `w06`
- **Explanation:** Pay gaps inside an organisation. Agreeing treats pay as a private contract; disagreeing thinks extreme gaps are a public problem worth capping.
- **Scoring (agreeing):** Economics +0.8 (→ Markets), Authority −0.35 (→ Liberty)

  - _If you agree:_ "…but the gap should at least be published openly" → Economics −0.2 (→ Equality), Decision +0.2 (→ The Experts)

### 7. Taxation takes what belongs to the person who earned it.

- **ID:** `w07`
- **Explanation:** This probes whether tax feels like a shared subscription or a confiscation. Agreeing leans toward a minimal state; disagreeing treats tax as the price of a society.

### 8. Unions hold a country back more than they help it.

- **ID:** `w08`

### 9. Housing is an asset like any other, and people are entitled to profit from it.

### 10. Most people who end up poor were dealt a bad hand, not a bad character.

### 11. Help from the state should come with something expected in return.

### 12. Money should be managed by specialists, well out of reach of whoever won the last election.

### 13. A country should live within its means, even when that means going without.

### 14. A society that produces billionaires has got something wrong.

- **ID:** `w14`

### 15. When prices spike on something essential, the state should step in and hold them down.

### 17. A country is better off when its people work hard and produce more.

# Power & the State (20)

_How much a government may ask of you._

### 19. If you have nothing to hide, being watched should not frighten you.

- **ID:** `p01`
- **Explanation:** Surveillance catches criminals and also builds a record of everyone's life. Agreeing accepts monitoring as the price of safety; disagreeing treats privacy as a right that does not need justifying.
- **Scoring (agreeing):** Authority +0.9 (→ Order)
- **Caveats:**
  - _If you agree:_ "…but only with a warrant signed by a judge" → Authority −0.5 (→ Liberty), Decision +0.25 (→ The Experts)
  - _If you agree:_ "…watching foreigners is different from watching citizens" → Borders −0.45 (→ Nation)
  - _If you disagree:_ "…and the same goes for corporations, not just the state" → Economics −0.4 (→ Equality)

### 20. Some questions are too serious to be settled by a public vote.

- **ID:** `p02`
- **Explanation:** Constitutions, rights, scientific questions, war. Agreeing wants certain things insulated from majorities; disagreeing thinks the people should be able to decide anything about their own country.
- **Scoring (agreeing):** Decision +0.85 (→ The Experts), Authority +0.4 (→ Order)
- **Caveats:**
  - _If you agree:_ "…because rights should not depend on a majority mood" → Authority −0.5 (→ Liberty), Culture +0.3 (→ Progress)
  - _If you agree:_ "…because most voters simply do not have the information" → Decision +0.35 (→ The Experts), Authority +0.3 (→ Order)
  - _If you disagree:_ "…though a vote should need a large majority, not a bare one" → Decision +0.3 (→ The Experts)

### 21. A country sometimes needs a leader who can cut through the process and act.

- **ID:** `p03`
- **Explanation:** The appeal of decisive rule against the friction of parliaments, courts and consultation. This is one of the clearest markers on the authority axis.
- **Scoring (agreeing):** Authority +0.95 (→ Order), Decision −0.35 (→ The People)
- **Caveats:**

### 22. Someone who leaks a government secret to expose wrongdoing is doing the right thing.

- **ID:** `p04`

### 23. Decisions should be made as close to the people they affect as possible.

### 24. Protest that shuts down ordinary life is still legitimate protest.

- **ID:** `p06`
